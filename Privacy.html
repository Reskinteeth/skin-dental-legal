# Skin Dental Privacy Policy **Effective Date: August 2, 2026** Skin Dental respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Skin Dental collects, uses, protects, and shares information when you communicate with us, visit our online pages, request information, schedule an appointment, or participate in our SMS messaging program. ## 1. Information We Collect Skin Dental may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including: * Your name * Telephone number * Email address * Preferred language * General service interests * Appointment preferences * Communication preferences * Messages, questions, and other information you submit to us * Information about how you learned about Skin Dental We may also collect limited technical information when you visit our online pages, such as your device type, browser type, IP address, referring page, and general interaction information. ## 2. How We Use Information Skin Dental may use collected information to: * Respond to inquiries * Provide information about Skin Dental services * Coordinate appointments and introductions to participating dental professionals * Send appointment confirmations, reminders, scheduling updates, and follow-up communications * Send promotional or marketing messages when the recipient has provided the required consent * Improve our services, communications, and marketing * Maintain business records * Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity * Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations Skin Dental is a marketing, lead-generation, supply, location, and introductory scheduling company. Skin Dental does not provide dental treatment and does not maintain clinical dental records. Please do not send medical histories, diagnoses, X-rays, clinical photographs, insurance records, or other sensitive treatment information through SMS or ordinary marketing forms. Clinical and treatment-related information should be provided directly to the treating dental professional through the method that professional designates. ## 3. SMS and Mobile Information When you provide your mobile telephone number and consent to receive messages, Skin Dental may send texts relating to: * Responses to your inquiry * Appointment coordination * Confirmations and reminders * Rescheduling or cancellation information * Service follow-up * Offers, promotions, and marketing communications, when separately authorized **Mobile information, including telephone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent, will not be sold, rented, shared, or transferred to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.** Skin Dental may provide mobile information to service providers that help us deliver or support communications, such as telecommunications, messaging, hosting, customer-support, and technology providers. Those providers may use the information only to provide services to Skin Dental and not for their own marketing. SMS consent is not transferable and will not be treated as consent to receive marketing from another company. ## 4. How We Share Information Skin Dental may share information in the following limited circumstances: ### Service providers We may use vendors that support our business operations, including communications, scheduling, website hosting, data storage, analytics, payment processing, and technical support. These providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to perform their services. ### Participating dental professionals When you request an appointment or introduction, Skin Dental may provide relevant contact and scheduling information to the participating dental professional who will communicate with or provide services to you. The dental professional is independently responsible for their clinical relationship with you and for their own privacy, recordkeeping, and legal obligations. ### Legal and safety requirements We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to: * Comply with applicable law, court orders, or legal process * Protect the rights, safety, or property of Skin Dental, its users, or others * Investigate suspected fraud, misuse, or illegal activity * Enforce applicable agreements and policies ### Business transactions Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of some or all of Skin Dental’s business, provided the recipient continues to protect the information consistent with applicable law. ## 5. We Do Not Sell Mobile Opt-In Information Skin Dental does not sell mobile telephone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or messaging consent. Skin Dental does not share mobile telephone numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their independent marketing or promotional purposes. ## 6. Cookies and Analytics Our online pages may use cookies or similar technologies to: * Operate and secure the pages * Understand how visitors use them * Measure marketing performance * Improve content and functionality Your browser may allow you to limit or block cookies. Some functionality may not operate correctly if cookies are disabled. ## 7. Data Security Skin Dental uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet transmission, text message, email, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should not use SMS or email to send highly sensitive medical, financial, or identification information. ## 8. Data Retention Skin Dental retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to: * Respond to inquiries * Coordinate services and appointments * Maintain business and communication records * Meet legal, accounting, and operational obligations * Resolve disputes * Enforce agreements Information may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer reasonably needed, subject to applicable legal requirements. ## 9.
